The Problem

Production happens fast.
Spreadsheets can’t keep up.

When deadlines loom, 3D teams lose critical hours searching for missing meshes, deciphering vague revision notes, and wondering which version the art director signed off on.

01 / VERSION DRIFT

Lost files & naming chaos

Asset iterations scatter across local drives and private chat threads. Nobody knows whether to texture v2_final.blend or v3_clean_APPROVED.blend.

“Which blend file has the non-destructive modifiers baked?”
02 / BLIND HANDOFFS

Unclear stage dependencies

Riggers start building skeleton hierarchies on geometry with unapproved edge-loops, while lookdev artists light models that fail polycount budgets.

“Rigging spent three days skinning a model that failed QA.”
03 / FRACTURED REVIEWS

Endless, scattered threads

Frame-accurate art feedback is buried across emails, video calls, and unindexed Slack messages. Notes get missed, forcing redundant revision cycles.

“The client asked to change the silhouette five revisions ago.”

01. Concept & Blockout

Establish volume proportions, polycount boundaries, and art direction briefs. Reference imagery is bound directly to the production ticket before 3D artists start geometry.

Target Poly Budget Under 30k Triangles
Primary Format SubD Bounding Box

02. Topology & Clean Meshing

High-poly sculpts and low-poly retopology. All assets are verified for manifold geometry, consistent normals, and clean quad edge-loops ready for deformation.

Mesh Verification Manifold / Zero N-Gons
LOD Generations LOD0, LOD1, Collision

03. UDIM & PBR Shading

Bake normal maps, ambient occlusion, and roughness. Shaders and textures are versioned in studio storage, keeping texture paths consistent across artist workstations.

Resolution Spec 4K Metal / Roughness
Color Space ACEScg / Linear sRGB

04. Kinematics & Skeletal Rig

Establish FK/IK bone hierarchies, custom deformation controllers, and vertex weight painting. Rigs are automatically validated against game-engine export targets.

Bone Hierarchy Humanoid / Mech IK
Weight Bounds Max 4 Influences / Vert

05. Interactive 3D Turntable QA

Lead artists and supervisors inspect the model in real time. Rotate, zoom, toggle wireframes, and review vertex counts directly in the browser with no DCC installation needed.

Inspection Engine Hardware WebGL PBR
Annotation Notes Timestamped Feedback

06. Approved & Production Locked

Once approved, the asset version is cryptographically locked and marked production-ready. Downstream lighting, layout, and VFX departments receive instant automated notifications.

Pipeline Status Production Locked
Automated Export Unreal / Unity / USD
